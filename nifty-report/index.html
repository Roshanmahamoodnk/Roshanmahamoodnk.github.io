What the calculation means

P(all gates) = P(G0) × P(G1 | G0) × … × P(G5 | G0…G4)

Multiplication uses the conditional chain rule; it does not require independence. Inputs must reflect the route and earlier outcomes. The model collapses common shocks and timing into these assumptions.

What it cannot tell us

No calibrated probability of strategy profit, investor return, ruin or a specific annual return is available. There is no justified probability-weighted business valuation yet.

At zero probability for any mandatory gate, the direct path ends. At 100% throughout, the full path passes by assumption—not by evidence.

14 / The personal-use test

A small trading account may not repay a research business.

Arithmetic illustration, not a return forecast

Simple fixed-capital annual arithmetic. No compounding, cash withdrawals, income tax, capital losses beyond the input, or owner time. A higher required return is not a recommendation to target it.

Decision rule for own use

  1. Evaluate future operating costs separately from sunk research spending.
  2. Size in integer lots using observed premium and date-effective lot size.
  3. Keep trading principal separate from salaries, licence deposits and shutdown cash.
  4. Continue only if the validated distribution and controls fit the account’s capacity to bear loss.
  5. Do not build an expensive sales organisation merely to justify past research expenditure.
At the default illustrative inputs₹5L × 20% = ₹1L/year before operating overhead. ₹10,000/month of operating cash is ₹1.2L/year. Even that assumed trading return would not cover operations.

The actual annual return is unknown. Negative-return and zero-return scenarios belong in the decision. The prior 0.5% per-trade risk and ~20.7% linear P95 drawdown approximation do not establish an enforceable loss ceiling.

18 / What development must deliver

Build a reproducible research system before a polished trading app.

Research layer

  • Immutable raw files and source manifests.
  • Canonical contract/date identity.
  • Causal features and event-driven replay.
  • Dated costs, integer-lot equity and exclusions.
  • Run hashes, tests and downloadable ledgers.

Execution layer

  • Broker-supported integration and unique order IDs.
  • Idempotency, position reconciliation and reconnect recovery.
  • Stale-price rejection and explicit no-trade state.
  • Daily/open-risk limits and kill switch.
  • Credentials separated from strategy/report files.

Customer layer

  • Entitlements and minimal account access.
  • Versioned claims and research disclosures.
  • Support ownership, incident and complaint records.
  • Billing, scope, renewals and refunds.
  • Capacity gates before onboarding more users.
Release / pause / retirement policy

Each deployed version has an owner, frozen configuration, dated evidence and rollback target. Material strategy changes return to research. A critical order or data defect pauses operation immediately; performance drift follows predeclared monitoring limits. Restart requires documented reconciliation. Retirement honours customer and data-retention obligations.

Information needed before a return or risk forecast

Final net trade ledger, complete signal/rejection ledger, price/fill assumptions, account capital and whole-lot rules, daily equity and cash flows. Use day/week block resampling with uncertainty and concentration analysis. Do not turn the old sum of trade percentages into CAGR or infer ruin probability from a single PF.

What to defer

Mobile apps, broad dashboards, multiple brokers, automated marketing, a large indicator library, Bank NIFTY expansion and extra strategy engines until the primary route passes its evidence and customer gates.

Model definitions

d, s, n, e, c, m are conditional pass probabilities for G0–G5. q is S2’s joint research-and-option pass probability conditional on accepted data and S1 failure.

A = d·s·n + d·(1−s·n)·q
P(final endpoint) = A·e·c·m
Reach = [1, d, d·s, A, A·e, A·e·c]
E(expense) = Σ Reachᵢ·Costᵢ + Reach₅·Extension + d·(1−s·n)·RetryCost

With retry off, q and RetryCost are zero. Entered-gate expense is charged in full; gate failure means no pass by the funded review boundary. A passed alternative uses the same downstream probabilities by explicit simplifying assumption.

Funding ceiling: all core expenses + retry cap + contingency + provisional deposit + selected trading capital + any extension. Expected expense excludes contingency use, blocked deposit and principal. Zero salvage and zero sales receipts are assumed in funding calculations.

Limits: no explicit correlation/time-to-event model, no income tax or financing, no calibrated adoption model, no live backtest and no investor NPV. Scenario ranges are not uncertainty intervals. Reported high PF cannot supply the missing probabilities.
